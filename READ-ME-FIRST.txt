VECTORIZER LOCAL - for Mac
==========================

Turns images (PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP, WebP) into editable, layered vector files
(SVG, PDF, EPS, DXF, PNG), matches hex colours to Pantone colours, and shows
logos as embroidered, woven, dye-sublimated or leather patch mockups.
Everything runs on your Mac. No internet and nothing to install.

Works on Apple Silicon (M1-M4) and Intel Macs, macOS 11 Big Sur or newer
(macOS 12 Monterey or newer recommended).


1. INSTALL
----------
Drag "Vectorizer Local.app" into your Applications folder
(or keep it anywhere you like).


2. OPEN
-------
Double-click "Vectorizer Local.app".

The first time on a new Mac, macOS may say it "could not verify" the app,
because it is not registered with Apple. That happens when the app arrives
by AirDrop, download, email or a chat app. To allow it, once:

  macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:
    1. Click "Done" in the warning.
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll down to "Vectorizer Local was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway".
    4. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open".

  macOS 14 Sonoma or older:
    Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose "Open", then click "Open".

  Or, in Terminal (any macOS version):
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Vectorizer Local.app"

After that it opens with a normal double-click.
Copying it with a USB stick or external drive usually avoids the warning.


3. USE
------
- "Vectorizer" (top left): drop, paste (Cmd+V) or choose an image. Adjust the
  palette, layers and editing tools on the right, then click "Download" and
  choose where to save.
- "Pantone match": type or paste hex codes (e.g. #E4002B) to see the closest
  Pantone colours. "Match palette to Pantone" in the Vectorizer opens it with
  the image's colours.
- "Thread match": type or paste hex codes to see the closest Isacord 40 and
  Madeira Polyneon 40 embroidery threads (number and name). "Match to thread"
  in the Vectorizer opens it with the image's colours.
- On both match pages, the Hex / RGB / CMYK buttons let you enter an RGB
  (0-255) or CMYK (0-100%) colour instead of a hex code. CMYK is read as
  printed with the Coated FOGRA39 press profile (ISO 12647-2, coated paper);
  CMYK made for another press or paper can differ.
- "Patch mockup": drop a logo (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP or SVG) to see it as an
  embroidered, woven, dye-sublimated or leather patch. Pick which colours are
  fabric or thread, the shape, border and look, then "Download PNG".
  For Faux/Leather, pick a Heritage Leatherette sheet (Lone Star Adhesive)
  under Look; an engraved logo shows the sheet's core colour. "Logo size"
  under Shape & border makes the logo smaller or larger inside the patch.
  The stitched seam can be dashed, dotted or a plain line, with its own
  thickness and colour (Stitch, Thickness and Colour under Shape & border).
  For an embroidered patch, choose Isacord 40 or Madeira Polyneon 40 under
  Colours and type a thread number under each thread colour (or click "Use"
  for the nearest thread).
  ("Patch mockup" in the Vectorizer sends the open image there.)
  "Choose logo" asks before replacing a patch you are working on.
  With Shape "Logo only", Outline can be None or a Filled outline (width and
  colour) around the logo. Faux/Leather engraves or presses a colour logo as
  one solid logo. "Download PDF" (Faux/Leather) saves a layered vector PDF:
  the shape in white with a thin outline, the logo in black and the stitch
  lines, each on its own layer.
- "Gang sheet": add one logo (PDF, AI saved PDF compatible, SVG, PNG, JPG,
  WebP or TIFF), choose the sheet size and the logo size, and the sheet is
  filled with as many logos as fit (turned 90 degrees when more fit). Gap,
  margin, a box round each logo and the sheet border can be changed.
  Download as PDF or AI (vector for PDF/AI/SVG logos), EPS, PNG, JPG or TIFF
  at 150-600 dpi. Click a logo on the sheet to take it off (click again to
  put it back); the round buttons beside the rows take off a whole row. The
  sheet keeps its size and the other logos stay where they are.
  Cmd+Z (Ctrl+Z) or the Undo button undoes the last change, up to 50 times.
  EPS logos cannot be read on a Mac (macOS has no PostScript
  reader any more): save them as PDF first.
- Close the window to quit.


Notes
-----
- Pantone matches use unofficial Pantone colour data (from Adobe colour
  books). Confirm final colours with a physical Pantone guide. Pantone's data
  is proprietary: keep this to personal or internal use. PANTONE is a
  trademark of Pantone LLC.
- Thread colours come from the DigitEMB Thread Color Matcher (digitemb.com,
  October 2026). A thread's screen colour is an approximation: check against
  a physical thread card before production. Personal or internal use.
- If something goes wrong, the log is in
  ~/Library/Logs/Vectorizer Local.log
